Process Architecture

Downstream Refining Architecture

Designed around modern conversion technology to handle diverse African sweet and sour crude blends.

⚗

Atmospheric & Vacuum Distillation

Initial separation stage splitting crude into fractions according to boiling points, feeding downstream secondary conversion units with maximum thermal efficiency.

🔥

Residue Fluid Catalytic Cracking (RFCC)

High-conversion cracking unit upgrading heavy bottoms into high-octane gasoline blending stock, automotive gas oil (diesel), and valuable propylene feedstock.

💧

Hydrotreating & Euro-V Compliance

Advanced desulfurization systems removing sulfur compounds, guaranteeing refined fuel meets strict Euro-V emissions standards (<10 ppm sulfur) for cleaner East African air quality.

🧪

Polypropylene & Petrochemicals

Integrated petrochemical complex converting refinery off-gases into high-grade polypropylene resins used across packaging, automotive components, and agricultural piping.

Refined Slate

Target Product Portfolio

Supplying Kenya and regional EAC states with high-demand transport fuels and industrial inputs.

Premium Motor Spirit (Gasoline)

Euro-V high-octane motor gasoline for passenger vehicles and commercial fleets across East Africa.

Automotive Gas Oil (Diesel)

Ultra-low sulfur diesel designed for heavy transport, commercial logistics, rail freight, and agriculture.

Aviation Jet A-1 Fuel

Clean aviation fuel meeting international IATA standards for Jomo Kenyatta (Nairobi), Moi (Mombasa), and regional airports.

Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG)

Clean household and institutional cooking gas reducing reliance on biomass, charcoal, and deforestation.

Strategic Logistics

Port & Pipeline Marine Terminal

A modern mega-refinery depends upon world-class marine logistics. Sited directly adjacent to Lamu Deep Sea Port, the project includes:

  • Single Point Mooring (SPM) Buoys: Offshore deep-water moorings for rapid crude unloading without channel congestion.
  • Subsea Pipelines: Dedicated multi-product undersea pipelines connecting marine berths directly into tank farms.
  • Strategic Petroleum Reserve Storage: Millions of barrels of finished fuel storage guaranteeing supply during global shortages.
  • Rail & Pipeline Terminals: Integration with the Kenya Pipeline Company (KPC) network and the LAPSSET rail corridor.

Refinery Infrastructure Specs

Site Footprint: Designated industrial zone, Mokowe, Lamu.

Power Generation: Dedicated captive multi-megawatt combined cycle power plant guaranteeing 100% operational uptime.

Water Desalination: On-site seawater reverse osmosis (SWRO) facility to safeguard municipal freshwater supplies.

Waste Heat Recovery: Cogeneration capturing thermal exhaust for steam generation and energy conservation.
