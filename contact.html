Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions concerning the project research portal and the Lamu refinery initiative.

The project has been planned as part of the broader industrial master plan for the Lamu Port-South Sudan-Ethiopia Transport (LAPSSET) corridor. Pre-feasibility studies, site zoning in Mokowe, and stakeholder engagements have been conducted to align with regional demand projections.

The 650,000 bpd refinery in Lekki, Lagos is the flagship operational installation of the Dangote Group in West Africa. The proposed East African Refinery in Kenya is envisioned to replicate similar downstream fuel self-sufficiency for the East African Community along the Indian Ocean corridor.
