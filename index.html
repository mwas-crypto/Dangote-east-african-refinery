Dangote East African Refinery

Exploring the development of one of Africa's major petroleum and petrochemical projects in Lamu County, Kenya.

Introduction

The Dangote East African Refinery is a major industrial investment led by Nigerian businessman Aliko Dangote through the Dangote Group. The project is located in Mokowe, Lamu County, Kenya. It is intended to produce petroleum products for Kenya and other countries in East Africa. The project aims to strengthen regional energy security, support industrial development and reduce dependence on imported refined petroleum products.

Expected Benefits to Kenya

Employment Opportunities

The project is expected to create employment opportunities during construction and operation, benefiting skilled and unskilled workers.

Economic Growth

The investment could support economic growth, increase industrial activity and encourage additional business opportunities.

Energy Security

Local refining capacity could reduce dependence on imported refined petroleum products and improve regional fuel supply.

Regional Trade

The refinery is intended to supply petroleum products to Kenya and neighbouring East African countries.

Challenges and Considerations

Environmental Impact

Refinery operations must address pollution, waste management, emissions and protection of marine ecosystems.

Project Financing

Financing such a large industrial project requires significant investment and effective financial management.

Land and Community Issues

Land rights, compensation and community participation are important considerations in the development of the project.
