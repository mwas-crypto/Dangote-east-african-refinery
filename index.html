Executive Background

Pioneering East African Energy Self-Sufficiency

The Dangote East African Refinery is an ambitious industrial investment led by Nigerian industrialist Aliko Dangote through the Dangote Group.

Strategically sited in Mokowe within Lamu County, Kenya, the facility is designed to process crude into refined petroleum products—including Euro-V gasoline, ultra-low sulfur diesel, aviation jet fuel, and petrochemical feedstocks—serving Kenya and the wider East African Community (EAC).

By establishing high-capacity domestic refining along the Indian Ocean coastline, the initiative aims to eliminate regional vulnerability to volatile global imports, lower logistics costs, and catalyze local industrial manufacturing.

Core Strategic Pillars

Strategic Coastal Hub

Direct integration with Lamu Deep Sea Port facilitating supertanker berth and seamless maritime distribution.

LAPSSET Corridor Anchor

Serving as a primary industrial anchor for the Lamu Port-South Sudan-Ethiopia-Transport corridor.

Petrochemical Derivatives

Supplying polypropylene and fertilizers to stimulate domestic agriculture and manufacturing across Africa.
